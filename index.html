Questions

Good to know before we start

What's the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Pressure washing blasts dirt off with force. Soft washing uses low pressure — about the strength of a garden hose — plus a cleaning solution that kills the algae, mold and mildew causing the stain. Roofs, siding and wood get soft washed. Concrete gets a surface cleaner. Using high pressure on the wrong surface is how shingles lose granules and siding gets water behind it.

Will the cleaner hurt my plants or grass?

Not when it's done right. We pre-wet landscaping, cover sensitive plants and rinse everything thoroughly during and after the wash. Damaged landscaping almost always comes from skipping those steps — we don't.

How often should I have my house washed?

In this climate, most homes look best on a yearly wash. Roofs generally go two to four years depending on tree cover and shade. If you're already seeing green or black, it's time.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we have access to water and the areas being cleaned. Most customers are at work. We'll send photos when we're done if you'd like.

How long does a job take?

Most single-family house washes run two to four hours. A driveway is usually one to two. Roofs vary with pitch and access, but nearly everything we do is a same-day job.

What does it cost?

It depends on square footage, surface type and how much growth we're dealing with. Estimates are free and we quote a firm price before we start — no hourly surprises.

What about payment?

Payment is due when the job is complete and you've approved the work. Cash, check and major cards accepted.
